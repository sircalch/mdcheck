Trajectory convergence, stationarity, and statistical independence were systematically assessed using MDCheck v1.0.0 (Monreal-Hernández, 2026). For all monitored observables (Backbone), the initial equilibration phase (up to t_eq = 14.11 ns) was automatically detected and discarded by maximizing the effective sample size N_eff of the production regime. Integrated autocorrelation times (tau_int) and statistical inefficiency factors (g) were evaluated via self-consistent Madras-Sokal windowing, ensuring a minimum of N_eff = 105 statistically uncorrelated conformations in the production ensemble. Stationarity and absence of systematic drift were confirmed using Geweke diagnostics and Flyvbjerg-Petersen block averaging. Reported values represent production means with 95% moving-block bootstrap confidence intervals.
